The Problem

Buying offers shouldn't live on paper scraps and gut math.

A scale and a pocket calculator aren't enough when you're buying gold rings, silver coins, and platinum scrap in the same transaction — with a customer waiting. One wrong purity grade or a stale spot price can cost you the deal or eat your margin.

Spot Pricing

Gold, silver, and platinum spot prices auto-fetched from gold-api.com and cached locally — falls back to manual override when offline.

Buy Calculator

Three Modes

List mode for multi-item buys grouped by category, Single for one item, Batch for identical quantities — pick the right tool for the transaction.

Coin & Bullion Library

Weight Presets Built In

Known coins and bullion products have preset weights so you only need to confirm quantity — no manual gram entry for common items.
