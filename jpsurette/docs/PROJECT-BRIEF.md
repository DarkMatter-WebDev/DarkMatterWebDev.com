# Project Brief

## Business

JPS Surette Photography is the portfolio and future event-gallery site for Jonathan Peter Surette, a Boston-based photographer focused on combat sports, architecture, real estate, editorial, and commercial assignments.

## Positioning

The brand lives at the intersection of physical intensity and structural precision: fight gyms, ringside moments, urban architecture, steel, glass, brick, and disciplined light.

## Audience

- Combat sports gyms, athletes, promoters, and brands.
- Fighters, coaches, teams, families, and event attendees looking for post-event photo galleries.
- Architects, real estate teams, developers, and commercial property clients.
- Professionals, brands, estate teams, product businesses, and select private event clients needing polished photography with a high-contrast edge.
- Editorial and commercial art directors looking for high-contrast Boston-rooted imagery.

## Product Direction

The long-term center of the site should be event galleries. After a fight or shoot, visitors should be able to find a gallery, log in or access it privately, view the images, and potentially download or purchase selected photography. Pricing may support free post-fight photo dumps, paid galleries, or a hybrid model.

## Visual Direction

- Dark, high-contrast, gallery-like presentation.
- Sharp-edged layout with no soft card styling.
- Heavy Montserrat headlines, Inter body text, JetBrains Mono metadata.
- Local image assets in `assets/` to avoid brittle remote URLs.

## Current Deployment Model

Static HTML/CSS/JS intended for Netlify. Netlify publishes the project root and captures the contact form through Netlify Forms.

## Asset Quality Rule

Do not compress, downscale, convert, or otherwise alter photography in a way that changes quality unless Jonathan explicitly approves that tradeoff. If optimization is needed, prefer lossless approaches or responsive delivery that preserves the original source files.
