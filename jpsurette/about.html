Jonathan Peter Surette

The Grit of the Ring. The Soul of the City.

JPS Surette Photography is a Boston-based studio documenting the intersection of high-stakes combat sports and architectural structuralism. The visual language is sharp, minimal, and physical: sweat, steel, brick, glass, and the geometry inside decisive moments.

Available for commercial shoots, fight coverage, gym features, real estate portfolios, architectural case studies, and editorial commissions.
