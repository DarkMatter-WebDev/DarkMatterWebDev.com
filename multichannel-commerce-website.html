Common questions

What buyers will want to know.

Is this another Shopify or marketplace template?

No. It is an independent branded commerce website built on a reusable custom foundation. The exact deployment, hosting, payment, and integration stack is selected for the business during scoping.

Will my business have its own website and domain?

Yes. Your branded website is the primary storefront and can use your business domain. Marketplaces extend your reach; they do not replace your home base.

Can I manage it from different computers?

Yes. The back office is browser-based, so authorized users can access it securely anywhere they have an internet connection.

How does the AI Listing Assistant work?

Start by uploading the product images. Then speak naturally about what the item is, its condition, history, materials, measurements, or anything else a buyer should know. The AI combines what it can recognize in the images with what you say and fills the appropriate listing fields for you. You review and edit the completed draft, confirm details such as price and shipping, and approve it before it is published to any channel.

Which marketplace connections are ready?

The working foundation supports live publishing to eBay and Etsy, plus Instagram and Facebook product and promotional publishing. The exact channels and account connections are confirmed in each project scope.

Do I have to recreate the listing for each channel?

No. The entire point is to enter the core product information once. Your website, eBay, Etsy, Instagram, and Facebook outputs are produced from that shared record, with channel-specific details handled in the same workflow.

Will my store look like Naples Estate Jewelry?

It can use the same proven commerce foundation, but it does not need to share the jewelry site's branding. Colors, typography, layout, categories, content, product fields, and workflows are tailored to your business.

Are marketplace accounts and fees included?

No. You maintain your own marketplace, payment, advertising, domain, and other third-party accounts. Their transaction, subscription, advertising, and usage fees remain separate.

Can the system support unusual products or selling methods?

Yes. The foundation can be adapted for one-of-a-kind goods, detailed specifications, offers, inquiries, appointments, auctions, evaluations, trades, consignment, and other specialty workflows.

How much does a complete system cost?

Pricing depends on catalog complexity, marketplace connections, custom workflows, content migration, design, payments, and ongoing support. We scope the business first, then provide a clear project plan and proposal.
