Consignor Spine

One consignor record connects floor items, auction lots, sales, and settlements.

Instead of keeping floor inventory and auction inventory in separate lanes, the app keeps the consignor as the center of the operation. Items can begin as floor stock, move into an auction event, return to the floor, sell, invoice, and settle while staying tied to the same consignor history.

Brand & Layout

Feature Requests

Add new modules for online bidding prep, estate sale intake, pickup scheduling, warehouse locations, SMS/email notices, or custom item categories.

Forms & Fields

Change item fields, consignor agreements, settlement rules, commission structures, buyer premium settings, and invoice language.

Tag Printing

Customize tag templates on the fly, including item numbers, lot numbers, barcodes, QR codes, prices, category fields, store branding, and print-ready label formats.

Reports & Exports

Create custom reports for accounting, taxes, staff reviews, consignor summaries, event recaps, inventory aging, and photo exports.
