The Problem

Precious metal signals are scattered across five different tools.

Reading RSI in one tab, MACD in another, checking spot price on a third site — there’s no single place that turns all those signals into a clear directional read. MetalsPredictor consolidates live pricing, chart analysis, and six technical indicators into one dashboard with a single Analytical Forecast score at the top.
