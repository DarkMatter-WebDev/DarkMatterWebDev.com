Common questions

What buyers will want to know.

How does the AI Listing Assistant work?

Upload the product images, then speak naturally about the item, condition, history, materials, measurements, or anything else a buyer should know. AI combines what it recognizes with what you say and fills the appropriate listing fields. You review, edit, and approve the draft before publishing.

Which connections are ready?

The foundation supports publishing to eBay and Etsy plus Instagram and Facebook product and promotional publishing. Exact channels and account connections are confirmed in each project scope.

Do I recreate the listing for every channel?

No. Your website, eBay, Etsy, Instagram, and Facebook outputs are produced from one shared record, with channel-specific details handled in the same workflow.

Will my business have its own website?

Yes. Your branded website is the primary storefront and can use your domain. Marketplaces extend your reach; they do not replace your home base.

Can I manage it from different computers?

Yes. The back office is browser-based, so authorized users can access it securely anywhere they have an internet connection.

Will my store look like Naples Estate Jewelry?

It uses the same proven commerce foundation, but colors, typography, layout, categories, content, product fields, and workflows are tailored to your business.

Are third-party accounts and fees included?

No. You maintain your marketplace, payment, advertising, domain, and other third-party accounts. Their transaction, subscription, advertising, and usage fees remain separate.

How much does a complete system cost?

Pricing depends on catalog complexity, connections, custom workflows, content migration, design, payments, and support. We scope the business first, then provide a project plan and proposal.
