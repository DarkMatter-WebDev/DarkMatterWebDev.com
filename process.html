BUSINESS SOFTWARE

Replacing the software you already run — without the risk.

When the project is custom business software, we usually step into a business that already has tools in place. We don't rip them out and hope — we study what's there, build the replacement beside it, prove it on your real data, and switch only when it's seamless.

  1. 01

    Come in & assess

    We start inside your business and map the software and systems you already have in place — how everything actually works day to day.

  2. 02

    Trim the fat, add what you need

    We remove what's slowing you down and keep what works — then build in the capabilities your team actually needs, including the exact features you've always wanted but your old tools never offered.

  3. 03

    Design a prototype

    We design a working prototype around your real workflows — not a generic template.

  4. 04

    Run it in place, on real data

    We get it 100% running and in place, then make copy migrations so we can test against your real data — side by side with your current tools.

  5. 05

    Switch only when it's seamless

    We make the switch only when everything's proven and ready, so the cutover is seamless — no downtime, no guesswork.
