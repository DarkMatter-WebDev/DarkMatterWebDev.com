New to all of this? Plain-English meanings of the words on this page

Website hosting — the service that keeps your website running on the internet, a bit like renting the space it lives in. If hosting stops, the website goes offline.

Domain name — your website's address, like YourBusiness.com. It's registered for a yearly fee, and it should be registered in your name — not your web company's.

SSL — the little padlock next to the address in a web browser. It scrambles information traveling between your visitors and your website so others can't read it.

Form and lead — a form is the "send us a message" box on a website. Each message a visitor sends is called a lead, and it's delivered to your email.

Backups — saved copies of your website, so it can be restored if something goes wrong.

SEO — setting the website up so search engines like Google can find and understand it. Good setup helps, but nobody can promise a #1 ranking — be wary of anyone who does.

What Happens After the First 12 Months?

This is the question everyone should ask before signing a managed plan — so here's the answer, in plain language.

You Own the Website

For a $0 upfront managed website, the website is built as part of a 12-month managed service agreement. After you complete the initial 12-month term, pay all required amounts, and have no outstanding balance — you own the completed website content and website design created specifically for your business.

Ownership does not mean that future hosting, updates, maintenance, technical support, or paid tools become free. Those continue only with an active plan or separate payment.

After the initial term, you can go three ways:

1. Continue With Us

Stay on the same managed plan at the then-current plan price. Hosting, updates, care, backups, monitoring, and support continue while the plan remains active.

2. Move to a Care Plan

Move to a lower or higher post-term care plan depending on the level of service you need going forward.

3. Request a Handoff

Request the available website files, content export, credentials, or account transfer needed to manage the site elsewhere. A standard handoff is included once the agreement is completed and the account is paid in full.

Renewal structure

After the initial monthly 12-month term, the managed plan automatically continues month to month unless you cancel with at least 30 days' notice.

Annual prepaid plans renew annually unless you change plans or give notice at least 30 days before renewal.

Pricing may continue at the current plan rate or a new published rate after reasonable advance notice.

Canceling after 12 months

You may cancel after the initial term by providing at least 30 days' notice. The account must be paid through the cancellation date. You may then request a handoff or transfer.

What about migration help?

A standard handoff is included after the agreement is completed and the account is paid in full. Active migration work — platform conversion, configuration on another provider, troubleshooting with a new host, or rebuilding features on a different platform — may be billed separately. Migration assistance starts at $250 or is billed at the applicable hourly rate, depending on complexity.

What if I cancel early?

If you cancel before completing the initial 12-month agreement:

  • The remaining balance of the initial agreement may become due
  • The website is not eligible for handoff until the required balance is paid
  • Hosting and service may end according to the service agreement
