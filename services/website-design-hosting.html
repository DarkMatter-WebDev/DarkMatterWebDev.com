Included in every build

Deployed and handed to you — your choice of hosting.

Already have hosting? We deploy your finished site there. Don’t have any? We set up a free, base-tier Netlify account in your name and deploy to that instead. Either way, we connect your domain, verify SSL, and hand over every credential with a walkthrough of what lives where and what renews. This is standard on every package below — there is no separate setup fee for it.

You are the account holder from day one, so nothing you rely on sits in a Surette Data Systems account. Business email and analytics are not part of the standard setup but can be added on request. Setup-related questions are covered for 14 days after handover; after that, further work is billed at the published $125/hr rate.

01

02

03

04
