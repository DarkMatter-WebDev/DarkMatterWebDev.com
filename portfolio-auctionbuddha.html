Under the hood

Everything above is the smallest part of it.

AuctionBuddha is closest to a localized eBay built for one vendor mall — a complete drop-in ecosystem, not a website with an auction page bolted on. Most of the build lives behind the login, where an admin center runs the online marketplace and the physical store from the same records.

storefront
VENDOR MINI-STOREFRONTS

Any vendor in the mall can open and run their own storefront inside the main site — their own inventory, listings, and buyers, under the mall's roof.

gavel
DUAL-PATH AUCTIONS

A vendor can fire an immediate timed auction on their own lots, or schedule those lots into the store's end-of-month house auction — two different workflows on one engine.

dashboard
ADMIN CENTER ECOSYSTEM

Vendor management, approvals, inventory, listings, orders, auction scheduling, and reporting — the operational core the storefront simply sits on top of.

forum
FULL MESSAGING

Messaging runs through the storefronts and the admin center, so the mall, its vendors, and buyers all talk inside the platform instead of in scattered email threads.

label
LABEL PRINTING

Tag and label printing is part of the system, so what is listed online and what is priced on the shelf stay the same record.

point_of_sale
RUNS THE STORE TOO

The platform integrates with and controls in-store operations, not just the website — one ecosystem covering the counter and the catalog.
