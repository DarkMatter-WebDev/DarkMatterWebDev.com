# 6. What Happens After the Initial 12 Months

Create a clear section explaining what happens after month 12.

Use plain language.

## Ownership After 12 Months

For a $0 upfront managed website, the website is built as part of a 12-month managed service agreement.

After the customer:

* Completes the initial 12-month term
* Pays all required amounts
* Has no outstanding balance

the customer owns the completed website content and website design created specifically for the customer’s business.

Ownership does not mean that future hosting, updates, maintenance, technical support, or paid tools become free.

## After the Initial Term, the Customer May:

### Continue With Surette Systems

The customer may continue on the same managed plan at the then-current plan price.

Hosting, updates, care, backups, monitoring, and support continue while the plan remains active.

### Move to a Different Care Plan

The customer may move to a lower or higher post-term care plan depending on the level of service needed.

### Request a Handoff

The customer may request the available website files, content export, credentials, or account transfer needed to manage the site elsewhere.

A standard handoff is included after the agreement is completed and the account is paid in full.

Active migration work, platform conversion, configuration on another provider, troubleshooting with a new host, or rebuilding features on a different platform may be billed separately.

Migration assistance should start at $250 or be billed at the applicable hourly rate, depending on complexity.

## Renewal Structure

After the initial monthly 12-month term, the managed plan should automatically continue month to month unless the customer cancels with at least 30 days’ notice.

Annual prepaid plans should renew annually unless the customer changes plans or gives notice at least 30 days before renewal.

Pricing may continue at the current plan rate or a new published rate after reasonable advance notice.

## Cancellation After 12 Months

The customer may cancel after the initial term by providing at least 30 days’ notice.

The account must be paid through the cancellation date.

The customer may then request a handoff or transfer.

## Early Cancellation

If the customer cancels before completing the initial 12-month agreement:

* The remaining balance of the initial agreement may become due.
* The website is not eligible for handoff until the required balance is paid.
* Hosting and service may end according to the service agreement.
