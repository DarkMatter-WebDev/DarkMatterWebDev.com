Marketplace Flow

From vendor storefront to item discovery to live auction interest.

What It Supports

A marketplace front end built around real antique-mall behavior.

  • storeVendor shop pages for independent sellers under one mall or marketplace brand.
  • categoryPublic browsing by jewelry, art, antiques, collectibles, vintage, coins, watches, and other categories.
  • sellListing detail pages with item title, price context, condition, vendor attribution, save/cart actions, and buyer-oriented presentation.
  • gavelA dedicated auction board for live lots with searchable and sortable auction discovery.
  • contact_mailContact workflows for buyer questions, seller messages, and management follow-up.

Best Fit

  • storefrontAntique malls with multiple vendors and rotating inventory.
  • diamondEstate jewelry, vintage watches, coins, antiques, art, and collectible dealers.
  • groupsLocal marketplaces that need a polished public catalog plus protected seller access.
  • campaignBusinesses that want listings, auctions, vendor onboarding, and contact capture in one web app.
