**Any overlay on a card face must be IN-FLOW — never positioned, z-indexed, transformed, will-changed, or opacity-transitioned** (2026-07-16, final form after three device round-trips; supersedes the interim state-hide pattern). In-flow content is rasterised into the face itself — like `.poke-badge`, which never glitched once across all three device tests — so it rides the card through the whole flip and is culled with the face at 90°, no state juggling or timing gates needed. `.tcg-stat-pill` is therefore fully static, placed over the art by layout: a zero-height flex row (`.tcg-stat-row`) that follows the art, with the pill pulled up by negative margin to the same 8px/8px corner insets.
