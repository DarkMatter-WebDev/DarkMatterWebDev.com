# Feature: Local On-Site Services

Last updated: 2026-06-01

## Summary

Dark Matter now has a local service category for practical in-person technology setup. This expands the brand beyond online-only website services while staying aligned with the "outsourced website/technology partner" positioning.

## Current Site Pages

- `services/in-home-services.html`
- `services/office-network-setup.html`

## Current Messaging

- In-home and on-site tech setup.
- Wi-Fi and networking.
- Device, printer, scanner, and workstation installation.
- Smart equipment and home office support.
- Small office network setup with clean handoff notes.

## Navigation

The desktop Services dropdown is grouped into:

- Online Services
- In-Home & Office Services

Homepage and services overview cards also expose the local category so it is not hidden inside the dropdown.

## Open Questions

- Define travel/service area.
- Decide starting price or estimate-only language.
- Clarify whether residential home support is fully offered or focused on home offices.
- Define what is excluded, such as electrical work, in-wall cabling, security camera installation, or managed IT contracts.
