# I18N Notes

Last updated: 2026-06-05

## Structure

- English pages live at the root.
- Spanish pages live under `es/`.
- Service mirrors live under `es/services/`.
- Keep page pairs synchronized for layout, nav, metadata, and CTAs.

## Language Switches

- English pages link to Spanish mirrors using `es/...`.
- Spanish pages link back using `../...` where needed.
- Keep `hreflang` alternates current.
- Local language preference may be stored in `localStorage` as `dm_lang`.

## Copy Rules

- Use natural Spanish, not literal word-by-word translation.
- Preserve legitimate Spanish punctuation, including opening and closing question marks where appropriate.
- Do not change Netlify form `name` attributes just to translate labels.
- Keep product names consistent:
  - Dark Matter
  - Dark Matter Web Services
  - Dark Matter Apps
  - Sean's Google Ads
  - Apps, not Downloads
  - Portfolio, not Case Studies in the main nav

## Path Rules

- From root pages, assets usually use `assets/...`.
- From Spanish pages, root-relative or `../assets/...` may be needed depending on page location.
- Before large bilingual edits, compare the English and Spanish mirrors for matching sections and nav.
