# I18N Notes

Last updated: 2026-07-03

## Structure

- English pages live at the root.
- Spanish pages are intended to live under `es/`, but the `es/` tree is currently absent from this working copy.
- Service mirrors are intended to live under `es/services/` when Spanish is restored.
- When Spanish mirrors are regenerated, keep page pairs synchronized for layout, nav, metadata, CTAs, form field names, and `data-message-source` values.

## Language Switches

- English pages should link to Spanish mirrors using `es/...` only when the mirrors exist.
- Spanish pages should link back using `../...` where needed once restored.
- Keep `hreflang` alternates current when bilingual pages are present.
- Local language preference may be stored in `localStorage` as `dm_lang`.

## Copy Rules

- Use natural Spanish, not literal word-by-word translation.
- Preserve legitimate Spanish punctuation, including opening and closing question marks where appropriate.
- Do not change public form field names or `data-message-source` values just to translate labels.
- Keep product names consistent:
  - Dark Matter
  - Dark Matter Surette Systems Portal
  - Surette Data Systems
  - Sean's Google Ads
  - Apps, not Downloads
  - Portfolio, not Case Studies in the main nav

## Path Rules

- From root pages, assets usually use `assets/...`.
- From Spanish pages, root-relative or `../assets/...` may be needed depending on page location.
- Before restoring or editing Spanish pages, compare the English and Spanish mirrors for matching sections, nav, metadata, form behavior, and Supabase message-source values.
