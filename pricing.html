Questions

The short answers.

Is there a setup fee?

Most projects launch with no setup fee — your first website build is included in your monthly plan. For larger or more custom builds, a one-time setup fee may apply, and we will always scope that with you upfront.

What if I need more pages?

We can accommodate any size. If your project exceeds the page count in your plan, we will scope a custom quote — no surprises.

Can I set up my own hosting instead?

Yes. Add Self-Hosting Setup and Handoff to any one-time build — $450, or $750 if your site stores form records. We create the hosting, domain, DNS, SSL, business email and analytics accounts in your name, deploy the site, hand over every credential, and walk you through it. You pay those providers directly, typically a few dollars a month. Setup questions are covered for 14 days; after that it is billed at $125 per hour. You can still hand it back to us on a care plan later if you would rather not run it.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no long-term contracts. Cancel anytime — no penalties, no hassle.

Do I own my website?

That depends on which route you take, and the difference is deliberate. On a monthly plan this is a fully managed service: your site stays live, supported, and continuously maintained as part of your subscription, and we hold the hosting account because hosting is part of what you are paying for. On a one-time build you own the finished website outright — and if you add Self-Hosting Setup, every account is created in your name, so you are the account holder and can move on without us. Either way, your own content — your logo, photos, text, and business information — is always yours.

What’s included in ‘support’?

Content updates, design tweaks, troubleshooting, and third-party integration help — anything related to keeping your site running and looking great. One Page and Starter are email support; Growth adds phone and chat; Pro adds a priority queue.

Can I pay annually instead?

Yes. Paying annually saves you two months on Starter, Growth, and Pro — $124, $207, and $374 a month respectively instead of $149, $249, and $449. One Page stays $65 either way.
