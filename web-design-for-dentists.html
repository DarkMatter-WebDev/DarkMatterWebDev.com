What makes Dentists different

Four things this site has to handle.

01

Two very different visitors

A broken tooth and a veneer consultation are not the same journey. Emergency routing and cosmetic storytelling both have to exist without crowding each other.

02

Insurance and financing decide it

Cost is the most common hesitation. Accepted plans, financing options and new-patient offers being easy to find removes the biggest barrier.

03

Cosmetic work sells visually

Before-and-after from your own cases is the most persuasive content a cosmetic practice can publish — with consent, and clearly your own work.

04

Anxiety is real

A meaningful share of visitors are nervous. Tone, sedation options and a plain description of a first visit matter more than they look.

Questions

How long until it is live?

A Landing Page is usually live in about 1–2 weeks once content and access are in hand. Larger builds run longer, and Fast Track is available if you are working to a deadline. Timelines are estimates measured from when we have everything, not promises.

Can we show before-and-after photos?

Yes, and they are usually the strongest content on a dental site. Obtaining patient consent is your responsibility; we build the gallery.

Can you connect our booking system?

We can connect a scheduling system you already use. Which system you run is your call.

Can we run a new-patient offer?

Yes, and changing it is a normal paid request afterward — you send the change, we make it, billed hourly.
