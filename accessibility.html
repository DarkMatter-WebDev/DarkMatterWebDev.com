Accessibility Statement

Surette Data Systems is committed to making surettesystems.com accessible to all users, including people with disabilities.

Effective date: June 2026

verified Our Commitment

We target conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities. We believe that everyone deserves equal access to information and functionality on the web.

pending Current Status

This website is partially conformant with WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Accessibility is an ongoing effort and some areas of the site may not yet fully conform. We are actively working to identify and resolve gaps.

info Known Limitations

We are aware of the following areas that are currently under active review and improvement:

  • keyboard Keyboard navigation — Some interactive elements, including dropdown menus and animated components, may not be fully operable by keyboard alone in all scenarios.
  • contrast Color contrast — Certain secondary text and decorative UI elements are under review to ensure they meet the 4.5:1 contrast ratio required by WCAG 2.2 AA.

code Technical Specifications

This site relies on the following technologies for conformance:

  • › HTML5
  • › CSS (including Tailwind CSS)
  • › JavaScript

The site is tested on modern versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge on both desktop and mobile devices.

feedback Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this site, please let us know. We take all feedback seriously and reply as soon as we can.

mail Contact Us call (239) 404-8505

gavel Formal Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response to your accessibility concern, you may contact the relevant enforcement body in your jurisdiction. In the United States, accessibility complaints related to websites can be directed to the U.S. Department of Justice Civil Rights Division or the U.S. Access Board.
