Benji - Internal Payroll Management System

Benji is a web-based payroll platform built for small organizations with hourly staff. It replaces spreadsheets and manual processes with a single, auditable system - professional but approachable, and ready to scale beyond a STEM education center.

The Problem

Payroll should not live in email threads and spreadsheets.

Hourly teams need a trustworthy path from time entry to approved hours, calculated pay, downloadable stubs, and exportable reports - without losing history when staff change roles or leave.
